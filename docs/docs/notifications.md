# Notifications

To turn on email notifications, click your name at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Notifications**. You can also open **Account** from the same menu and click the **Notifications** tab.

Make sure **Enable Email Notifications** is turned on. You must verify your email address before Exceptionless sends you email notifications.

Under **Project Notifications**, select the project you want to edit notifications for, and then turn on the notifications you want to receive for that project: **Daily Project Summary**, **New Errors**, **Critical Errors**, **Error Regressions**, **New Events**, and **Critical Events**. The event notifications require a paid plan. Changes save automatically.
