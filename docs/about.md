Exceptionless is a privately held software company, established in 2013, specializing in tools that help developers fight errors in their apps. The name says it all: *exceptionless* means **to be without exception**.

## Open from the start

We open sourced Exceptionless in 2014, and the hosted service still runs the same code you'll find [on GitHub](https://github.com/exceptionless/Exceptionless). Along the way we've built and shared other open source projects like [Foundatio](https://github.com/FoundatioFx/Foundatio), the building blocks behind Exceptionless's queues, caching, jobs, and storage.

## Our vision

Build the best developer tools available, so developers around the world can get their jobs done faster and more efficiently.

## Our mission

We believe in being open and available to our customers and listening to what they have to say. Our highest priority is the success of our customers, followed closely by continued innovation in our products.
